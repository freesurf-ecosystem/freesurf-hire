## Invoices
                                            
Create professional invoices in seconds — free, forever.

Invoices is a free, open-source invoice generator for independent contractors, freelancers, and small businesses. No subscriptions, no watermarks, no account required to get started. Just fill in your details, preview your invoice live, and download it as a polished PDF.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Create professional invoices in seconds
• Live preview as you type
• Download as a PDF on any device
• Add your logo, business details, client info, line items, tax, and discount
• Save drafts and access previous invoices across devices when you sign in
• Your business info is saved to your account, so you never retype it

WHY INVOICES?
• 100% free — no subscriptions, no paywalls
• Open source — GPL v3 licensed; free to use, modify, and share
• No account needed — start invoicing immediately
• Works everywhere — sign in to sync drafts and invoices across devices

Built for people who work for themselves


## Reader

Listen to any text out loud — free and open source.

Reader turns any text into natural speech. Paste or import text, pick from 20 natural voices across 9 languages, and listen instantly. No account, no setup, no per-use fees.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Paste any text or import PDF and TXT files
• 20 natural voices — American, British, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, German, Hindi, Polish
• Adjustable playback speed (0.75x–1.5x)
• Seek, jump, and track your progress
• Save recordings locally for replay

WHY READER?
• 100% free — no account, no hidden costs
• Open source — GPL v3 licensed; free to use, modify, and share
• Private by design — your text is used only for the TTS request, never stored
• Self-hosted AI — runs on our own GPU, no third-party API fees

Read on your own terms.


## Transcriber

Turn any audio into text with speaker labels — free and open source.

Transcriber converts speech to text with speaker diarization, so you can see not just what was said, but who said it. Record or import audio and get a full transcript with speaker labels, timestamps, and plain-text export. Built for meetings, interviews, podcasts, and conversations.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Record audio or import files
• Speaker-labeled segments with timestamps
• Toggle between speaker view and plain text
• Edit the transcript inline
• Copy and share
• Save your last 50 transcripts locally

WHY TRANSCRIBER?
• 100% free — no account, no per-minute fees
• Open source — GPL v3 licensed; free to use, modify, and share
• Private by design — audio is processed and immediately deleted, never stored
• Self-hosted AI — runs on our own GPU, no API subscriptions

Built for meetings, interviews, podcasts, and conversations.
