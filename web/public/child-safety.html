FreeSurf has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to preventing, detecting, and removing any content or activity that involves or facilitates the abuse or exploitation of minors, and to cooperating with the authorities and organizations responsible for protecting children.

What is prohibited

Our standards prohibit, without limitation:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form, including images, video, text, or links;
  • Any content that sexualizes minors or depicts the sexual exploitation of minors;
  • Grooming behavior, and any attempt to solicit, contact, or exploit a minor for sexual purposes;
  • Content that promotes, encourages, or normalizes the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.

Prevention and moderation

We take reasonable steps to detect and prevent CSAE on our platform, including moderation of user content, review of reports, and removal of offending material. We are committed to continually improving these safeguards.

Reporting

If you encounter suspected CSAE or any content that exploits or endangers a minor, report it immediately using the in-app reporting tools where available, or contact us directly at support@freesurf.tools. We review reports promptly and take action in accordance with this policy.

Enforcement

Accounts and content that violate these standards are subject to immediate action, which may include removal of content, suspension, or permanent termination of accounts. Where required, we report CSAE to the relevant authorities and organizations, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement, and we cooperate with their investigations.
