AI Processing: Some FreeSurf tools use AI to generate results. Before any of your content is sent to an AI provider, the app asks for your permission and explains what is being sent. FreeSurf is currently subscription-first and does not run third-party advertising. AI content is sent only to the provider required to produce the result you requested and is transmitted through our own servers. This section explains where AI models run, what we send to them, and what happens to the results.

Where AI models run. We choose the AI infrastructure that best fits each tool and may switch providers over time. AI processing runs on one or more of the following:

  • Together AI â€” a hosted, serverless GPU inference provider (used for most of our models today; Together Computer, Inc., together.ai).
  • OpenRouter â€” a hosted inference aggregator (used for some tools and fallbacks).
  • Self-hosted RunPod â€” our own rented GPU infrastructure (for specific models we run ourselves).

We generally prefer open-source models (for example, open-weight text, vision, speech, and text-to-speech models). We use a closed-source model only when necessary to achieve an acceptable result for a particular feature.

What we send to an AI provider. Depending on the tool, the inputs you provide are sent to the active provider's infrastructure solely to generate the result you requested:

  • Natural Reader: text you ask to be read aloud.
  • Transcriber: audio you ask to be transcribed.
  • Calorie Tracker: food photos and/or food descriptions.

Your inputs are shared only with the provider(s) running the model for that request, strictly to produce the output. We do not use them to sell to or profile you, and we instruct providers not to use submitted content to train their models.

What happens to the results. The result returned by the AI provider is delivered back to you in the app. Depending on how you use the tool, results may be stored locally on your device (for example, generated audio or saved recordings) and, if you are signed in, associated with your account and stored in our database so your history can sync across devices. Where a result is associated with a user, it is subject to the same access, retention, and deletion rights described in this Privacy Policy.

No training. Content you submit is never used to train or fine-tune our models, and we do not permit the providers we use to train on it for their own benefit.

Data minimization. We send only the information required for the specific request, we do not combine your AI inputs with unrelated personal data, and we rely on providers that delete or do not retain submitted content beyond what is required to fulfill the request. Together AI and our other providers process this content only to complete the request and provide data protection that meets or exceeds our own standards.

6. Data Retention & Your Choices

  • Local data: Data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account data: Retained while your account is active. You can delete your account by contacting us.
  • AI processing: Submitted text, audio, and photos are sent to the active AI inference provider to produce a result and are not retained there beyond the request. Generated results are saved locally on your device; if you are signed in, they may also be stored in our database and associated with your account for cross-device access. See the AI Processing section above.
  • No model training: User content processed by our AI models is never used to train, fine-tune, or improve the models. Transcripts, audio, and images are used strictly for immediate real-time output.
  • Support requests: May be retained to resolve issues and document outcomes.
  • Contractor profiles: Retained while your profile is published and until you ask us to remove it. Profile content is public, so third-party copies (including search engine caches) may persist independently.
  • Client requests: Retained so the contractor you contacted can follow up and keep a record of the job, then deleted.
  • Consent records: Retained for up to 3 years from acceptance to evidence the agreement and defend claims, then deleted. See section 1.

7. GDPR & CCPA Rights

These rights apply to your consent records as well, including the IP address and user agent stored with them. If you have a public contractor profile, note that we can remove it from FreeSurf, but we cannot remove copies that search engines or other third parties have already made.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@freesurf.tools. We will respond within 30 days. For EEA users, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
